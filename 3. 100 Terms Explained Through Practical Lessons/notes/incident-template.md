## Timeline
Record timestamp, observation/action, actor and evidence link for every meaningful event.

## Evidence
Expected vs actual behavior, error/status, request ID, relevant logs/metrics/events, artifact identity and recent changes. Redact secrets and personal data.

## Hypotheses
For each: evidence for, evidence against, bounded experiment and actual result.

## Mitigation and validation
What restored service? What user-facing operation proved recovery? What monitoring window was observed? Was any data lost?

## Contributing conditions
Trigger, technical factors, missing safeguards and process conditions. Avoid blame and unsupported conclusions.

## Actions
For each: specific change, owner, due date, priority and verification. Include runbook/test updates.

## Communication and closure
Audience, update timestamps, final recovery evidence and unresolved risks.
